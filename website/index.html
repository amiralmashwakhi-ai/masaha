Masaha: surveying services in Jordan, with trust

Masaha connects landowners and contractors with licensed surveyors and surveying offices, and protects both sides: a signed agreement for every job, secure escrow payment, and a complete job record.

How it works

  1. The customer submits a request with the plot location.
  2. Surveyors send quotes; the customer reviews their CVs and ratings.
  3. Both sides sign the service agreement electronically.
  4. The customer pays the agreed price into Masaha escrow.
  5. 30% is released after field work and 70% after the customer accepts the deliverables. Masaha's commission is deducted from the provider's payout.
